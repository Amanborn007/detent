TICKET OR TOPIC · THE ONE-LINE IDENTIFIER

A title that states the finding, not the subject

One or two sentences a reader can stop at and still be right.

1 · The short version

Bolded takeaway first. Then the reasoning, in whole short sentences. Lead with the answer, never the status.

2 · Evidence

Put the finding in the sentence and the anchor at the end of it, like this: the pill comes from one field only, mapper.go:890. Never a column of bare file anchors carrying the meaning.

Someone else's words go here, attributed. — Name, where, when

Your own aside — a caveat, a nuance, something the reader should carry but which is not the main line.

3 · Options

A table only where the shape carries the meaning. Comparing options across the same few dimensions is exactly that shape.

Recommendation, in one line, with the reason in the same breath if it changes what the reader does.

4 · A diagram

Every fill and stroke reads a token, so the diagram follows the page into dark mode. Copy this shape; do not paste hex literals into an SVG.
